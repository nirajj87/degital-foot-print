TXT record to add at your DNS host when the report says DMARC is missing.

Type:  TXT
Host:  _dmarc
Name:  _dmarc.yourdomain.com
TTL:   3600
Value: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com; fo=1; adkim=s; aspf=s; pct=100

Create inbox dmarc@yourdomain.com (or change rua). After reports look clean, you can use p=reject.
Check: nslookup -type=TXT _dmarc.yourdomain.com
